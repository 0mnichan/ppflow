PSF-20 Printer

Not connected

Text is drawn as an image, so any font or script your computer can show will print. 48 mm wide.

EAN-13 needs 12 or 13 digits, UPC-A 11 or 12, Code 39 capital letters and digits. These use the printer's own commands.

The test prints an even checkerboard. If you see skipped or garbled bands, lower the chunk size or add a delay below.

Transfer settings

Chunk 20 always works. Over a good link 100 to 185 is usually fine and much faster.
